Kyung Hee University Global Campus 경희대학교 국제캠퍼스
Geospatial digital twin · Yongin-si, Gyeonggi-do · WGS84 / EPSG:5186 · 1:1 scale

Kyung Hee campus buildings stand on their real OSM footprints and DTM ground and are shown with stylised colour and façades: windows are drawn for each estimated storey, the main gate, library and plaza obelisks are modelled from photographs, and the amphitheatre seats follow the real slope. The view is cropped to the university grounds. The terrain is a real bare-earth elevation model (FABDEM, 1″ ≈ 30 m), not a generated surface. Building footprints come from OpenStreetMap, cross-referenced with the official KHU campus map. Each building height is labelled documented derived or estimated.
