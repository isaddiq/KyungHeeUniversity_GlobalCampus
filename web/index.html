경희대학교 국제캠퍼스
Kyung Hee University Global Campus, Yongin. A 3D digital twin at 1:1 scale

The terrain uses FABDEM bare-earth elevations (1″ ≈ 30 m) with estimated local grades for playing fields and entrances. Building footprints come from OpenStreetMap and are cross-referenced with the official KHU campus map. Selected façades are modelled from photographs. The amphitheatre seats follow the terrain. Each building height is labelled documented derived or estimated.
